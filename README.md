# DDR Sandbox

A historically grounded open-world sandbox / immersive-sim prototype about everyday movement, surveillance, social systems and moral choices in the GDR. The project must distinguish documented history from reconstruction and fiction.

## Product thesis

Build a dense, legible historical sandbox rather than a map-sized spectacle. The strongest version combines environmental storytelling, social simulation, restricted movement, material scarcity, observation pressure and player-authored routes through a historically researched space.

## Initial vertical slice

- One compact urban/peri-urban district
- Third-person exploration
- Checkpoints, permits, suspicion and social reputation
- Civilian NPC schedules
- Basic surveillance / reporting system
- Inventory and scarcity loop
- One multi-solution mission with no perfect moral answer
- Explicit provenance for historical claims, objects and places

## Guardrails

- No invented historical claim may be presented as fact.
- Reconstructions must name their represented year or range.
- Real people require careful sourcing and privacy/defamation review where applicable.
- The simulation is not a political propaganda engine; competing lived perspectives should be represented through sourced context.

## Technical direction

Godot 4.7.1, desktop-first. World streaming and systemic simulation should be tested on a small region before scale-up.
